Two Kitchens, One Family Name

A little flour. A lot of Sandhya.

Founded in 1992 in Pala, Sandhya Caterers began with a simple promise: every wedding feast, housewarming and parish celebration should taste like it came from a generous family hearth, never an industrial assembly line.

Six years later, in 1998, Sandhya Bakers opened its doors so families wouldn't have to look elsewhere for their betrothal cakes, Christmas plum cakes, coconut-oil banana chips, and evening tea puffs.

Message Our Desk
How far in advance should we book wedding or reception catering?

For peak wedding and festive seasons (December to February, Easter, Vishu and Onam), we recommend booking 4 to 8 weeks in advance. For smaller family gatherings of 50 to 150 guests, 7 to 10 days is usually sufficient.

Can I pre-order a custom birthday or tiered wedding cake?

Yes. Share your date, weight (from 1 kg upwards), flavour preference and reference design on WhatsApp. Cakes can be picked up at Pala, Bharananganam or Murikkumpuzha, or delivered directly to your reception venue.

Do you provide serving staff, banana leaves and buffet counters?

Yes. Our catering quotes include uniformed serving staff, fresh banana leaves or porcelain crockery, chafing dishes, welcome drink counters and post-meal dining hall cleanup.

Can we order bulk Plum Cakes or Banana Chips for wedding return gifts?

Absolutely. We pack custom wedding return sweet boxes, 500g/1kg Christmas plum cakes, and vacuum-sealed Nendran chip packets for outstation guests.
